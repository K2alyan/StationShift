WHAT THE EXPERIMENT FOUND

More context helps.
It does not always win.

Chronos-2 reached within 10% of its own 90-day MAE with 3 days at +1h and 7 days at +6h/+24h. At +24h, its pooled MAE was 58.43 with 30 days of context, versus 62.33 for global LightGBM and 76.44 for persistence.

The 30-day result is a measured comparison, not a test-selected deployment recommendation. Chronos’s 90-day context was worse at the two longer horizons.

READ THE BUDGET CAREFULLY

Context is different
from fitting data.

Chronos uses rolling context without parameter updates. Global LightGBM learns from the other 11 stations; global+local adds a frozen pre-test fitting window. These curves compare allowed window lengths, not total readings acquired after one physical deployment.

0h is supported only by the donor-trained calendar model. High PM means a target above the development 90th percentile (168 µg/m³), with no health or regulatory interpretation. Pretraining overlap is uncertain.
