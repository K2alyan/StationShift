Under New-Station Distribution Shift

When does a pretrained time-series foundation model become competitive at a monitoring station held out from task-specific training?

Each Beijing station is held out in turn. Chronos-2 receives increasing PM2.5 context without task-specific fitting. LightGBM learns from the other 11 stations. This compares new-station transfer strategies under different information resources.

KEY FINDING

More context helps.
It does not always win.

Chronos-2 reached within 10% of its own 90-day MAE with 3 days at +1h and 7 days at +6h/+24h. At +24h, its pooled MAE was 58.43 with 30 days of context, versus 62.33 for global LightGBM and 76.44 for persistence.

Chronos’s 90-day context was worse at +6h and +24h. The 30-day comparison is descriptive, not an optimized deployment recommendation. Pretraining did not establish superior local-data efficiency.

INTERPRETATION

Context is different
from fitting data.

Chronos uses rolling context without updating parameters. Global LightGBM learns from the other 11 stations; global+local adds a frozen pre-test target-station fitting window.

The curves compare allowed information windows, not identical total observations after one physical deployment. Beijing pretraining overlap remains uncertain.
